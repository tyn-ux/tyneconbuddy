How a stop works

A chapter is 5 to 7 stops plus a finale. Every stop has the same six steps, so you only learn the routine once. Each stop matches one EconMon in the game.

~15 s🎬 HookOne or two lines about a real Hong Kong place and time.
~20 s🤔 你覺得呢？Guess first, before any teaching. No penalty for a wrong guess.
~30 s📖 Key ideaThe exam definition plus at most 4 short points. English term kept.
~30 s🔍 ExampleThe idea worked through in 3–4 numbered steps.
~20 s✅ CheckOne new question to check yourself.
~15 s🗣 Say itPick the best explanation of three. The two wrong ones are common misconceptions.

⏩ Fast track

If your guess is right, you can skip straight to the Check. Right again, and the stop is done. If you already know a chapter, you can finish it in about 6 minutes; if it is new to you, take the full path.
